# PAID Pilot — Your Personal AI Delegate, Set Up for You

> Hi 👋 — thanks for letting us pilot PAID with you this week.
>
> **What you're getting**: a personal AI assistant that handles routine
> inbound IM on your behalf — like a delegated EA who answers the small
> stuff and pings you when something needs your call. Three outcomes:
> auto-answer, ask-you-first, or hand-back-to-you. You always stay in
> control.
>
> **What you do**: brief us the way you'd brief a new hire — a
> 30-minute conversation about how you work and what you'd be happy
> delegating. That's it.
>
> **What we do**: everything else. Install, configure, run, monitor,
> debug, fix. Think of Jimmy as your interim CTO for this week.

---

## How this works for you

| You handle | We handle |
|---|---|

## §1. The briefing call (≈45 min, Lark voice or video)

This is the only setup investment from your side. Three parts.

### Part A · Authorise the integration in your Lark (≈10 min)

There's one tiny piece of clicking only you can do, because it
happens inside *your* Lark workspace — Lark requires the workspace
owner to authorise external integrations. **Jimmy will share his
screen with step-by-step instructions and walk you through each
click.** You'll be done in 10 minutes.

(If you happen to be in the same Lark workspace as Jimmy, even this
step disappears. Tell Jimmy your workspace and he'll check.)

### Part B · Brief us on your voice + what you'd delegate (≈30 min)

This is just a conversation. Jimmy asks, you answer, Jimmy takes
notes and turns them into PAID's brain.

We'll talk about:

#### How you want PAID to sound

Things like: "I write in Chinese unless they write in English." "I'm
short and direct, no exclamation marks." "When unsure, I'd rather
say 'let me check' than guess." Whatever's true for you.

#### What you'd be OK delegating

Walk through 3-5 questions colleagues / friends commonly ping you
about. Example: "people ask my office hours / address — fine to
auto-answer." "People ask if I can meet next Tuesday — should check
with me first." "Anything about pricing or hiring — must escalate,
never auto-answer."

#### What's absolutely off-limits

"Never discuss compensation." "Never confirm a commitment without
checking with me." "Never disclose introductions in flight." Etc.

After the call, Jimmy writes everything into PAID. You don't see
code, config, or files.

### Part C · Pick your first 1-2 test friends (≈5 min)

Name 1-2 colleagues / friends who've agreed to send you a few real
questions this week (you don't need to have told them yet — Jimmy
walks you through what to say to them too).

For each, Jimmy needs their Lark contact — handled live on the call,
takes 1 minute per friend.

That's it. By the end of the call PAID is live and watching for
their messages.

## §2. During the week (≈5 min/day)

Use Lark like you normally do. PAID quietly handles inbound from the
friends Jimmy set up. You'll see three things happen in your Lark
chats:

**🟢 Auto-answered** — PAID replied for you. You can see it inline
in the chat history (nothing hidden — you're in the same chat). If
something looks off, just follow up with a correction yourself; tell
Jimmy at end of week.

**🟡 Needs your call** — PAID DMs *you* a card: "Friend asked X.
Here's my draft answer. Approve / Edit / Reject?" You tap one and
it goes out. If you don't answer in 30 min, PAID tells the friend
"Jimmy will get back to you directly" — so nobody's left hanging.

**🔴 Handed back to you** — PAID told the friend "I'm not authorised
to answer this, please ping Jimmy directly." Your cue to handle
manually.

### What we want you to flag

Anytime over the week, just DM Jimmy when you notice:

1. PAID gave a **wrong answer** (what was the question, what was
   PAID's reply, what would you have said)
2. PAID **escalated something you'd have trusted it to handle**
3. PAID **answered something you'd have wanted to handle yourself**
4. A friend reacted with surprise — "huh?" or "are you a bot?"

These four classes are exactly what we need to tune. **Be brutal.
Negative is more useful than positive.**

### Things you don't need to do

- Look at logs ─ Jimmy watches them
- Edit anything ─ DM Jimmy what you want changed, he changes it
- Restart anything ─ Jimmy handles it
- Worry about data ─ everything is isolated to your private account
  on Jimmy's server (see §4)

## §3. The end-of-week debrief (≈30 min)

Friday or Sunday, we hop on a final 30-min call. Just three questions:

1. What 3 things worked well?
2. What 3 things didn't?
3. Would you keep using it? If yes, what would you want changed first?

This call is **the single most valuable thing you give us this
week** — more than the day-to-day usage. Honest negative feedback >>
polite positive. We want PAID to be useful for you specifically, and
the only way to get there is unfiltered reactions.

---

## §4. Privacy & control

- **Your data stays on a dedicated isolated account on Jimmy's
  server.** Separate from his and the other pilots' instances.
- **PAID only sees**: messages your test friends send to you via the
  Lark bot. PAID has zero access to your other Lark chats, your
  files, your calendar, your email.
- **At end of pilot**: three options, your call:
  - Keep PAID running (we hand over keys + a how-to)
  - Have us archive everything and shut down
  - Have us delete every trace — no questions asked
- **Source code is MIT-licensed and fully public**:
  https://github.com/jimmyag2026-prog/paid-plugin

---

DM Jimmy any time and say "let's stop." We shut down your instance
in under a minute. No questions, no debrief required, no awkwardness.
